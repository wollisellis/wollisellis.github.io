I am a Visiting Research Fellow at the ARTORG Center, AIHN Lab, University of Bern, through a FAPESP BEPE grant, and an M.Sc. candidate at Unicamp in the Multidisciplinary Laboratory in Food and Health (LabMAS). My research connects clinical nutrition with the rigorous evaluation of generative AI and knowledge graphs. I study how these tools can be made safe, transparent and useful for dietitians and patients.
