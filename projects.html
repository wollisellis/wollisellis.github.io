Projects

Current research lines in nutrition and health AI.

Brazilian Food Knowledge Graph (BFKG)

An extension of the Swiss Food Knowledge Graph (SwissFKG) to 50 core Brazilian institutional recipes, linked to TBCA 7.3 food composition, Ministry of Health and CECANE-UFMS sources and POF criteria. A modular Python pipeline compares lexical TF-IDF with EmbeddingGemma semantic retrieval across 9 language models under matched context budgets. Developed at the AIHN Lab, University of Bern.

NutriBenchBR & MADiMa

A pre-registered benchmark of 12 language models on 1,352 Brazilian nutrition exam items from 62 exams (16,224 model evaluations), reported under the TRIPOD-LLM guideline. A related zero-shot study on Brazilian Portuguese exam questions is accepted at MADiMa, ACCV 2026, Osaka.

Generative AI for Clinical Nutrition

Research on AI-assisted meal plan generation that combines retrieval-augmented generation with deterministic clinical safety rules and automated evaluation harnesses. The blinded evaluation with dietitians is published in Nutrition. This line is the basis of the M.Sc. thesis at Unicamp.

Multimodal Food Question Answering

Error analysis of retrieval, portion size estimation and USDA FoodData Central alignment in food visual question answering, covering 20 meal images, 50 questions and 140 USDA records. Carried out at the AIHN Lab, University of Bern.

Most research code and data are in private repositories because they involve unpublished manuscripts, reserved evaluation material and institutional agreements. Public material will be added once it is cleared for release. Public work is listed on GitHub.
