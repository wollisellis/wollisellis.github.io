Journal article, 2027

Blinded professional ratings of artificial intelligence-generated and dietitian-designed meal plans

Abhulime EWM, da Cunha DT, Monaco VR, de Melo RD, Benatti FB

Nutrition. 2027, volume 153, article 113428 (published online October 16, 2026)

DOI

Abstract

A web-based blinded trial in which dietitians rated AI-generated and dietitian-designed meal plans. Across 54 eligible dietitian records (130 evaluations, 124 complete), AI-generated plans received higher mean ratings (3.67 versus 3.11, Cohen's d = 0.86, paired t = 5.32, p below 0.001).
